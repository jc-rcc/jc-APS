🎁 13th Month Pay Computation (RA 8515) — 2026
EmployeeClassificationDate HiredStatusMonths in YearAvg Monthly PayProration13th Month PayPayout Status
Formula (RA 8515 / DOLE): 13th Month Pay = Total Basic Salary Earned During the Calendar Year ÷ 12. Prorated by actual days of service for employees hired or separated mid-year. Everyone who rendered service during the year is entitled — no minimum-length rule. Payment on or before December 24. Not subject to SSS, PhilHealth, or Pag-IBIG. Tax-exempt only up to the configured annual limit (default ₱90,000/year, RA 10963 / CREATE Act); any excess is taxable income subject to BIR withholding.
⚖️ Government Compliance Settings — 13th Month Exemption Limit (RA 10963 / CREATE)
13th Month Pay + other taxable bonuses are income-tax exempt only up to this annual ceiling. Any excess is taxable income subject to BIR withholding. Update this whenever the government amends the exemption amount so the system stays compliant. All existing tables below are also editable and persist across sessions.
⚙️ Configurable Bonus Types
Define additional bonus structures that can be applied to future payroll cycles. Mark a bonus as 🌿 De Minimis (BIR RR 11-2018) to make it tax-exempt, excluded from the ₱90,000 exemption pool, and exempt from SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG.

🧮 Payroll Basis — Working Days per Month
Divisor used to price one absence day for monthly-paid (Non-Teaching) staff
Formula: Gross = Monthly Salary − (Absences ÷ 22 × Monthly Salary). The standard convention is 22 working days per month — a fixed divisor keeps the daily absence value consistent across all months, since actual Mon–Fri days vary (20–23) and holidays remain paid for monthly-rate employees. Teaching staff are paid hourly and are not affected by this setting.
ℹ️
Important Reminder: Government contribution rates are subject to change. Always verify current rates with the official SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR portals before processing payroll. Quarterly audits are recommended.
